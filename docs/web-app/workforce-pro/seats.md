# Workforce Pro seats

A **Workforce Pro seat** marks a member whose wellness and workplace exposure records your organization keeps. Seats come from the [packs](./index.md#prices) you buy: each pack holds 25 seats. Records about a member can be added or changed only while that member holds a seat. Officers who record things for others need their wellness role, not a seat of their own.

**Where to find it:** **Subscription and Billing → Workforce Pro → Manage Workforce Pro seats**, or the **Manage Workforce Pro seats** link in the notice on a wellness page. Department admins only. The page exists while Workforce Pro is available to your department (the `Workforce.Pro` flag is on).

## Switch Workforce Pro on for members

1. Open **Workforce Pro seats**. The counter at the top reads, for example, *12 of 25 seats in use, 13 available*.
2. Find the member in the list. Members who have Workforce Pro switched on are listed first.
3. Select **Switch on**. The page says *Workforce Pro seat updated.* and the member's **Workforce Pro seat** column shows **On**.
4. To take a member off, select **Switch off**.

Every change is in the [audit log](../security-permissions.md#audit-logs) (*Workforce Pro Seats Changed*).

## What the seat column shows

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **On** | The member holds a licensed seat. Their records can be added and changed. |
| **On, not licensed** | Workforce Pro is switched on for the member, but there are more members switched on than seats (for example after buying fewer packs following a lapse). Members are licensed in the order they were switched on. This member's records stay read-only until you add a pack or switch someone else off. |
| **Off** | No seat. The member's existing records stay readable, and nothing new can be recorded about them. |

## Rules

- **Only active members** hold a seat. Deleted, disabled and hidden members cannot: *Only active members can hold a Workforce Pro seat; deleted, disabled and hidden members cannot.* A member who stops being active keeps their row, but it no longer counts, and the page notes how many seats belong to members who are no longer active.
- **No more than you bought.** Switching a member on when every seat is in use is refused with *All 25 seats are in use. Packs to add: 1.* and an **Add a pack** link to the billing page.
- **Switching off never deletes a record.** The member's records become read-only. You can switch a member off at any time, even while the add-on is not active.
- **Without an active add-on** nobody can be switched on. The page says *Your organization does not hold the Workforce Pro add-on right now. Existing wellness records stay readable; buy packs to add or change records.*
- **Packs follow the seats.** You cannot remove packs below the number needed for the members switched on. Switch members off first, then lower the packs on the billing page.
- Seats belong to the department. A member of two departments needs a seat in each one that keeps their records.

## Who needs a seat

Give a seat to every member whose exposure log, exposure cases, injuries or medical clearance you keep. The officers who run the program (wellness officer, designated officer) need a seat only if you also keep records about them.

| Organization | Typical seats |
|---|---|
| **Combination fire department** | Career and part-time staff who are employees; volunteers too if you track their exposures |
| **EMS agency** | All field clinicians (needlestick and infectious-disease exposure cases) |
| **Industrial emergency response team** | Brigade members who wear respirators or work with hazardous substances |
| **Search and rescue** | Team members, mainly for critical incident entries and peer support reminders |
